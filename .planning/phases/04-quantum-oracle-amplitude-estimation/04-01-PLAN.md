## Decisions this plan implements (recorded here; the SUMMARY carries them to PROJECT.md Key Decisions)

- **PD-15 (locked, research)**: keep the direction register. Q1 = 12 qubits, layout x(5) a(1) phi(4) e(1) T(1), qubit i = bit i of exact.py's `s = x | a << 5 | phi << 6 | e << 10`, flag T = qubit 11 (T = x4 OR (NOT x4 AND a AND e)). Q0 = 5 qubits, flag = qubit 4. Q1lite (8 qubits: x(5) a(1) e(1) T(1), escape angle averaged over the 16 bins) exists only as the IonQ size-ladder fallback; its strings carry no direction, so Phase 3's `sample_exit_rows` cannot consume them. Every model's flag is its highest qubit.
- **PD-16 (locked, research)**: Grover operator = `grover_operator(oracle=Z on the flag, state_preparation=A)` (the function; the class is deprecated in Qiskit 2.1). Compose the logical A·Q^k first, then ONE `transpile(c, basis_gates=BASIS, optimization_level=1, seed_transpiler=1)` with `BASIS = ["cx", "rz", "ry", "rx", "h", "x", "sx"]`. Never transpile A and Q separately (k = 1 gives 0.958 instead of 0.9994), never `transpile(c, backend)` (the IonQ target leaves an 11-control mcx; the Aer target leaves `multiplexer` and segfaults Aer at k = 16).
- **Contract gate (ROADMAP SC1)**: `make_problem(model, kernels=None)` computes the Statevector flag probability and raises `ContractError` when it differs from `exact.transmission_within(kernels, K)` by more than `CONTRACT_TOL = 1e-12` (K = 0 for Q0, 1 for Q1 and Q1lite). Runners (04-02) and main.py (04-03) take their circuits only from `make_problem`.
- **Dependency approval (orchestrator, Phase 1)**: qiskit-aer==0.17.2, qiskit-algorithms==0.4.0, ruff==0.16.10 and matplotlib are the pyproject `[dev]` pins Hippolyte approved in the Phase 1 dependency checkpoint (01-01-PLAN Task 1 list; 01-01-SUMMARY "Checkpoint record (Task 1)": reply `approved`).
