## Decisions this plan implements

- **PD-27 (orchestrator design 2)**: exit histograms use S = 1,000,000 matched samples. Seeding: `ss_classical, ss_rows, ss_aer = SeedSequence(seed).spawn(3)`; classical `run_lattice(kernels, S, ss_classical)`; quantum `AerSimulator(method="statevector")` with `seed_simulator = int(ss_aer.generate_state(1, dtype=np.uint32)[0])` on `BackendRunner(backend, problem, measure="all").circuits([0])[0]`, count keys sorted ascending, strings expanded with `np.repeat` in that order and passed to `sample_exit_rows(kernels, strings, ss_rows)`. 17 categories (16 rows + no back-face exit). Two-sample chi-square for equal totals = sum (c - q)^2 / (c + q) over categories with c + q > 0, dof = categories used - 1; goodness of fit = sum (c - S p)^2 / (S p) over p > 0; bound dof + 5 sqrt(2 dof) (the project's Phase 3 convention). Per-row z-scores are never computed (WR-07; 03-REVIEW IN-07: residuals in edge rows are statistical, never blamed on the kernels).
- **PD-28 (orchestrator design 3)**: truncation cases in this order: transport_corrected at 6, 10, 15, 20, 30 cm, then isotropic_raw at 10 cm; `SeedSequence(seed).spawn(6)` in case order; 20,000,000 histories each; bias SE = transmission SE (T1 is exact); coverage = T1 / T_full; the 10 cm transport-corrected coverage is the "~99.7%" disclosure, and isotropic_raw (about 89.9%) is shown only as a disclosure point, never as a model the circuit uses.
- **PD-29 (orchestrator design 4)**: thickness walls (6, 10, 15, 20, 30) cm with `boundary_x` 2.0 cm; per wall `child = SeedSequence(seed).spawn(5)[i]`, `ss_classical, ss_analytic = child.spawn(2)`, then the 05-01 series functions with R = 100, the 13-point classical grid, M = 1..9 at 300 shots; target = 0.01 T1 (relative RMSE 1%), costs by `analysis.cost_at_target` on each measured curve, ratio = N* / N_A*; also the fixed absolute target 1e-3 (disclosure); underflow guard `check_transmission(T1, max(classical_N), min_events=1000)`. Aer is not repeated per wall (the headline cross-check covers the circuit; every wall's oracle passes the statevector contract).
- **PD-30 (from 05-01)** applies to all three renderers (palette, allowlisted calls, no twin axes: two measures always go in two panels).
